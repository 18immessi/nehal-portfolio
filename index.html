Editing with intention.

I've been editing for over three years, working with YouTubers and Instagram creators to bring their ideas to life.

It's never just about making a video look good — it's about the hook, the pacing, the retention, the story. Every cut is a decision about whether someone keeps watching.

My main tool is DaVinci Resolve, but I adapt quickly to whatever workflow — or software — a project needs.
