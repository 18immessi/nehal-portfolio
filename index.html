Editing with intention.

I've been editing for over three years, working with creators and building videos across YouTube, short-form and social media.

It's never just about making a video look good — it's about the hook, the pacing, the sound, the story and the feeling behind every cut.

My main tool is DaVinci Resolve, with a workflow that covers editing, Fusion, motion graphics, sound design and colour. I also adapt quickly to whatever a project needs.
