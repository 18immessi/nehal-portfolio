Put your video thumbnail images in this folder.

Recommended sizes:
- Horizontal videos: 1280 x 720 (16:9), JPG or PNG
- Vertical videos:   1080 x 1920 (9:16), JPG or PNG

After adding an image here, open index.html, find the matching project card
(search for "project-1.jpg", "project-2.jpg", etc.) and change that filename
in the style="background-image:url('...')" part to your new file's name.

You can delete this text file once you've added your own images.
