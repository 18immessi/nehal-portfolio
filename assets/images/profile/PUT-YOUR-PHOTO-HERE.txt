Optional: put a profile photo here if you'd like one on the site.

Recommended: a square-ish photo, at least 800 x 800px, JPG or PNG.

To use it:
1. Save your photo into this folder.
2. Open index.html and find the "OPTIONAL PROFILE PHOTO" comment in the
   About section.
3. Delete the <div class="photo-placeholder">...</div> block.
4. Uncomment the <img> line just above it and set src to your photo's
   filename, e.g. src="assets/images/profile/nehal.jpg"

You can delete this text file once you've added your own photo.
